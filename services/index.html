How we work

We’re a small, senior team. There’s no account-management layer and no juniors doing the real work behind a senior pitch. Pricing is fixed upfront, so scope is a conversation rather than a surprise, and we reply on WhatsApp and Slack quickly.

We use AI tools where they make the work better and faster, and keep them out of the places where accuracy and ownership matter, like logos, product labels and claims. We’ve written up exactly how that works.

Who we work with

D2C brands, EV and energy companies, SaaS, hospitality and creator-led businesses, from pre-launch to post-Series A, in India, the UK, the US and elsewhere. See the brands on our clients page, or read about the studio.

Frequently asked questions

How much does branding cost?

A starter brand identity system starts at ₹25,000 and includes brand discovery, logo design, a colour and typography system, social profile assets and mini brand guidelines with source files.

Do you manage Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads?

Yes. Meta Ads management is ₹8,000 a month with ad spend billed separately, or take the ₹15,000 a month Creative + Meta Ads bundle, where creative and media buying are built together.

Is ad spend included in the price?

No. Ad spend is billed separately and paid to Meta. Our fee covers the setup, optimisation and reporting work.

Does Social Media Creative include posting?

No. It is design only: 8 custom static or carousel creatives a month, with no posting, captions or community management.

How many revisions do I get?

Social Media Creative and Campaign Creative include 2 revision rounds.

Where are you based and who do you work with?

We’re based in Gurugram, India and serve clients worldwide: D2C brands, EV and energy, SaaS, hospitality and creator-led businesses, from pre-launch to post-Series A.

How do we get started?

Book a free 1:1 session on WhatsApp or DM @_nmxlabs on Instagram. Pricing is fixed upfront and we reply fast.
