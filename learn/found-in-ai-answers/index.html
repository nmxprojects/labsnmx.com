Get found in AI answers

A free course on generative engine optimisation (GEO) for small brands: how AI search tools pick sources, how to write answer-first pages, and the proof, structure and freshness that get you quoted in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI answers.

Free, no sign-upBeginner friendlyQuiz after every lessonBy Neeraj, NMX Labs

Start lesson 1

The lessons

  1. 01

    How AI search picks what to quote

    When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google’s AI for the best design studio or the cheapest way to run Meta ads, the answer is built from web pages. Here’s roughly how those pages get picked, and the first things to check on your own site.

    Lesson 1 · 5 min read · quiz included
  2. 02

    Write answer-first pages

    The old blog habit was a long story first and the answer somewhere near paragraph nine. Readers hated it, and AI tools skip it. Put the answer at the top, then earn the scroll.

    Lesson 2 · 4 min read · quiz included
  3. 03

    Proof, structure and freshness: why AI trusts some pages

    Two pages say the same thing. One gets quoted, one gets ignored. Usually the difference is proof (who says so and how they know), structure (can a machine tell what’s what) and freshness (is this still true).

    Lesson 3 · 5 min read · quiz included

What you’ll learn

  • AI search tools look things up on the web, pick a handful of pages, then summarise them. If they can’t find or crawl your page, you can’t be quoted.
  • Answer the main question in the first one or two sentences, then add the detail.
  • Show your proof: named authors with real expertise, sources linked for every claim, and first-hand evidence like tests and your own numbers.

Questions

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimisation: making your pages easy for AI search tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI features to find, trust and quote.

Is this GEO course free?

Yes, free and without sign-up. Three short lessons and a quiz.

Does GEO replace SEO?

No. It builds on it. Pages still need to be crawlable and indexed; GEO is about being clear, credible and quotable once they are.
