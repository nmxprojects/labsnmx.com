Creator school

A free YouTube course for beginners: shoot on the phone you already have, fix light and sound for almost nothing, edit for free, write titles and thumbnails people click, and learn how creators get paid.

Free, no sign-upBeginner friendlyQuiz after every lessonBy Anisha and Neeraj, NMX Labs

The lessons

  1. 01

    You don’t need an iPhone: shoot your first video on the phone you already have

    Most first videos fail on light, sound and nerves, not on the camera. Here’s how to get a clean, watchable first video out of a ₹12,000 Android phone, plus the three cheap things worth buying later.

    Lesson 1 · 6 min read · quiz included
  2. 02

    The recipe for a good video: hook, promise, payoff

    Good YouTube videos look different on the surface and follow the same recipe underneath. Here’s the three-part structure we use for every script, with a worked example you can copy.

    Lesson 2 · 6 min read · quiz included
  3. 03

    The rules to follow on YouTube: guidelines, copyright, AI and paid promotions

    Most channels that get into trouble didn’t mean to break anything. Here are the rules that catch new creators in India most often, explained in plain language, and what to do instead.

    Lesson 3 · 7 min read · quiz included
  4. 04

    Light and sound on a budget: what to fix before you buy a camera

    A ₹40,000 camera can’t rescue a dark face and an echoing room. A bedsheet, a window and switching off the ceiling fan often can. Here’s our budget playbook for both.

    Lesson 4 · 6 min read · quiz included
  5. 05

    How to shoot educational videos people actually finish

    Teaching on camera is a different skill from teaching in a room. The four formats that work for explainers, how to choose one, and the small production habits that keep learners watching to the end.

    Lesson 5 · 6 min read · quiz included

    Your first edit: free editors that work in India, and cuts that keep people watching

    CapCut has been blocked in India since 2020, so here’s what to use instead on phones and computers, plus the five edits that matter more than any effect.

    Titles and thumbnails that earn the click without lying

    Your title and thumbnail decide whether anyone presses play. How to make them specific, readable on a phone and honest, plus how to test them without guessing.

    Lesson 7 · 6 min read · quiz included
  8. 08

    Shorts and long videos: how to make them help each other

    Shorts bring new people; long videos keep them. How the two formats differ, how to connect them, and a simple weekly mix for a small channel.

    Lesson 8 · 5 min read · quiz included
  9. 09

    How YouTube creators get paid, and what changes on 1 February 2027

    The YouTube Partner Programme’s entry bar for ad earnings doubles for new applicants on 1 February 2027. Here’s what the requirements are, what’s changing, and the income that doesn’t depend on them.

    Lesson 9 · 6 min read · quiz included
  10. 10

    Reading YouTube Analytics: five numbers and a 30-minute weekly routine

    YouTube Studio shows you dozens of charts. Five of them answer almost every question a small channel has. Here they are, what “good” looks like, and the weekly routine that turns them into decisions.

    Lesson 10 · 6 min read · quiz included

What you’ll learn

  • Any phone from the last few years records 1080p video that is good enough for YouTube.
  • Open with the hook: the problem or result, in the first few seconds, before any intro.
  • Community Guidelines strikes expire after 90 days; three active strikes end a channel.
  • Big, soft light flatters faces: a window or a white wall bouncing light beats a bare bulb.
  • Teach one idea per video and say what the viewer will be able to do by the end.
  • CapCut is banned in India. VN, DaVinci Resolve, Clipchamp and iMovie are good free options.
  • Plan the title and thumbnail before you film; they’re the promise.
  • Shorts are vertical and can be up to 3 minutes; they’re for discovery.
  • Fan funding (memberships, Super Thanks and more) starts at 500 subscribers.
  • Impressions click-through rate tells you whether the title and thumbnail work.

Questions

Is the YouTube course really free?

Yes. Every lesson, quiz and game is free, with no sign-up. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

Do I need an expensive camera?

No. The course is written for the phone you already have, plus a few cheap extras if and when you want them.

How long does it take?

About an hour in total. Each lesson takes five or six minutes and ends with a three-question quiz.
