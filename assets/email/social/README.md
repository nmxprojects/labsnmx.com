Round 64 x 64 icons for the newsletter's "Follow NMX Labs" row (shown at 32 x 32). Glyphs come from the Simple Icons
set (simple-icons on npm, CC0 public-domain licence), drawn white on an NMX ink circle. The brand marks themselves belong
to their owners; using them to link to your own profiles is the normal, allowed use. LinkedIn isn't in that set, so the
email shows LinkedIn as a small text button instead.
