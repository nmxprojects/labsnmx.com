About us

A studio built for teams that measure everything.

NMX Labs is a global creative & performance marketing studio based in Gurugram, India, serving founders and brand teams worldwide. We sit at the intersection of brand and performance — designing identities, campaign creative and Meta Ads systems that don't just look good in a case study, they move the numbers on the dashboard.

What we do

Brand identity, social & campaign creative, and end-to-end Meta Ads management — as standalone projects or as an integrated creative + media system.

How we work

Small, senior team. No account layer, no juniors doing the actual work. Fast Slack/WhatsApp turnarounds and pricing that's fixed upfront so scope is a conversation, not a surprise.

Who we work with

D2C brands, EV & energy, SaaS, hospitality and creator-led businesses — from pre-launch to post-Series A — who care about brand clarity and performance in equal measure.
