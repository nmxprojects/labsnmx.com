WhatsApp Business pricing changed in October. Here’s why Click-to-WhatsApp ads matter more now

Service replies on the WhatsApp Business Platform are no longer unlimited and free in India. Conversations that start from an ad still come with a 72-hour free window.

By Neeraj, NMX Labs · AI Design Expert29 October 20263 min read
Summarise with Google AI (opens Google AI Mode in a new tab)

Quick summary

  • WhatsApp Business Platform moved to per-message pricing in 2025, and from 1 October 2026 service messages in India are charged beyond a free allowance.
  • A chat that starts from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad opens a 72-hour window of free messages if you reply within 24 hours.
  • The ad itself is still paid; the saving is on the follow-up conversation.
  • Fast replies, a clear first message and good tracking decide whether CTWA campaigns pay off.

Summary by Neeraj. Prefer an AI take? Ask Google AI to summarise it (opens in a new tab).

Pricing on the WhatsApp Business Platform changes often and differs by country and provider. Check Meta’s official pricing page and your business solution provider’s current rate card before you plan budgets.

For many Indian businesses, WhatsApp is the sales channel. Customers ask questions there, negotiate there and pay from there. So when Meta changes what it charges for WhatsApp Business Platform messages, it changes the economics of selling.

What changed

In July 2025 the WhatsApp Business Platform moved from charging per conversation to charging per message delivered, with different rates for marketing, utility and authentication templates. Replies to customers within the 24-hour customer service window stayed free.

From 1 October 2026, that last part changed in India. Service messages are no longer unlimited and free: providers report a monthly free allowance per business number, with a per-message charge after that. For a business that handles thousands of support chats a month, that’s a new line on the bill.

This applies to the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API used through providers and CRMs). The free WhatsApp Business app on a phone works differently.

What didn’t change: the 72-hour free window

Meta still offers a free entry point. When someone messages you by tapping a Click-to-WhatsApp ad on Facebook or Instagram, or the WhatsApp button on your Facebook Page, and you reply within 24 hours, a 72-hour window opens. During those 72 hours every message you send is free, including marketing and utility templates.

“…for the following 3 days (72 hours), all of your messages are not charged.”

WhatsApp Business, Platform pricing

The ad is still paid like any other Meta ad. What you save is the cost of the conversation that follows, which is exactly where a sale is usually made.

When CTWA beats a website ad

  • Considered purchases where people have questions first: furniture, jewellery, courses, services, healthcare appointments.
  • Customisation: gifting, made-to-order products, bulk orders.
  • Trust-sensitive buyers who want to talk to a person before paying, often outside the big metros.
  • Thin or slow websites, where a chat converts better than a checkout page.

For simple, low-price products that people buy on impulse, a fast product page usually still wins.

Setting up a campaign that sells

  1. Connect the right number. Link the WhatsApp Business account you’ll actually answer from, and decide who answers during which hours.
  2. Write the pre-filled first message. Make it specific: “Hi, I’d like the price for the 6-seater teak dining set.” Specific first messages tell you which ad brought the person in and let you reply faster.
  3. Design the ad for a conversation. The creative should create a question worth asking, such as “Prices depend on size, ask us on WhatsApp”, not try to close the sale inside the ad.
  4. Reply fast. The free window depends on replying within 24 hours, and buyers go cold within minutes. Set up a quick automated greeting, then a human.
  5. Use the 72 hours well. Send the catalogue, answer questions, follow up with an offer before the window closes, all without per-message charges.

Measuring it properly

Ads Manager will show you messaging conversations started. That’s not revenue. Track each chat through to a sale in a sheet or CRM, by ad, so you can compare cost per sale with your website campaigns. Without that, CTWA campaigns look either brilliant or useless depending on who is reading the report. The budget maths in our Meta budget guide applies here too: work back from what a customer is worth.

What to do this month

  • Check your provider’s updated rate card and last month’s service message volume.
  • Move the conversations you most want to start (enquiries, consultations, bulk orders) to Click-to-WhatsApp ads.
  • Review your automated replies so customers aren’t sent more messages than they need.
  • Make sure every chat can be tied back to the ad that started it.
