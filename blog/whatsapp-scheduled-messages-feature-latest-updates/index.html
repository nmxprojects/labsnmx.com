WhatsApp can now send messages while you sleep. What else has changed?

WhatsApp has announced scheduled messages, a long-requested feature that lets people write a text, photo or video now and choose when it should arrive in an individual or group chat. Messages can be scheduled from 10 minutes to 14 days ahead, and WhatsApp says they will still be delivered if the sender’s phone is switched off. The update arrives during a busy year for WhatsApp, with new tools for groups, calls, events, security and travel.

Key takeaways

  • WhatsApp announced scheduled messages on 25 November 2026. Users can choose a delivery time from 10 minutes to 14 days ahead [1].
  • The feature supports text, photos and videos in individual and group chats. Users can review and delete queued messages before delivery [1][2].
  • WhatsApp says scheduled messages remain end-to-end encrypted and will send even if the sender’s phone is off or out of battery [1].
  • Recent updates include travel eSIM support, richer Events, guest calling, group-chat tools, passkeys and WhatsApp Web calling [3–9].

How WhatsApp scheduled messages work

The idea is simple: write a message when you think of it, then choose when it should reach the other person. WhatsApp says the feature works in one-to-one and group chats, with a scheduling window of 10 minutes to 14 days. It supports text, photos and videos1.

To schedule a message, open the chat, write your message and press and hold the send button. Choose the date and time, then confirm. The recipient sees the message like any other WhatsApp message. You can view upcoming scheduled messages and delete one before it goes out if your plans change12.

One detail makes this more useful than a simple reminder: WhatsApp says the message will still be delivered at the chosen time even if the sender’s phone is switched off or has run out of battery. The company also says scheduled messages remain end-to-end encrypted1.

The feature is rolling out globally over the coming weeks. That means it may not appear on every account immediately. Keeping the app updated is sensible, but the rollout itself is gradual12.

What else has changed on WhatsApp in 2026?

Scheduled messages are the headline, but the wider product story is just as interesting. WhatsApp has been adding features that make the app more useful for organising plans, calling people who do not have an account, and managing everyday conversations.

26 November 2026: Travel eSIMs

WhatsApp announced a way to buy and install a travel data eSIM from within the app through eSIM providers and telecom partners, including Jio, Smart Axiata, Smart Communications and Verizon. The company says the service will begin rolling out in select countries, so availability in India should not be assumed until it appears in the app3.

5 November 2026: Events beyond the group chat

WhatsApp expanded Events with an Events Home, customised invitations, reminders and plus-ones. People without WhatsApp can also open an event link, see details and RSVP. The update is designed to make planning easier without requiring every guest to join the app4.

29 October 2026: More useful group chats

WhatsApp announced group-chat upgrades including group Status updates, message history for new members and tools to help people catch up on conversations. These changes build on earlier group features such as polls and @all mentions56.

14 October 2026: Guest calling

Guest calling lets people join a WhatsApp call without creating a WhatsApp account. The host creates a call link and admits guests from a waiting room. Meta says calls can include up to 32 participants, with end-to-end encryption and no time limit7.

25 August 2026: Passkeys and account security

WhatsApp said more than one billion people had set up passkeys. The same announcement covered stronger two-step verification passwords and additional context for calls from unknown numbers, reinforcing the app's focus on account protection8.

4 August and 28 July 2026: Group tools and Web calling

Group updates added poll end times, hidden voter names, a 15-minute poll-edit window and @all mentions. WhatsApp Web calling brought audio and video calls to the browser, alongside call transfer and noise suppression features69.

22 July and 29 June 2026: iPad, car features and usernames

WhatsApp's July feature roundup covered iPad sign-up, refreshed CarPlay and Android Auto experiences, and PDF tools on web and desktop. In June, WhatsApp opened username reservations, giving users another way to connect without immediately sharing a phone number1011.

13 May and 26 March 2026: AI and everyday account tools

Earlier updates included Incognito Chat with Meta AI, storage management, easier account switching, chat-history transfer and AI-assisted creative and reply tools. These features show WhatsApp expanding its utility beyond messaging alone1213.

What the changes mean for users and brands

For everyday users, scheduled messages can handle birthday wishes, reminders and messages across time zones. The ability to review and delete queued messages is worth using: a message that made sense yesterday may not make sense when its delivery time arrives.

For businesses, the feature may help with routine one-to-one follow-ups or team coordination. But it should not be treated as a full campaign scheduler or a replacement for WhatsApp Business tools. Customer messaging still needs clear consent, appropriate frequency and a workflow that fits the business account.

The wider lesson is that WhatsApp is becoming a more complete communication platform. It is investing in planning, calling, privacy, security and cross-device use, not just adding another way to send a text.

Quick answers

How do I schedule a message on WhatsApp?

Open an individual or group chat, write your message and press and hold the send button. If the feature has reached your account, choose the date and time and confirm12.

How far ahead can I schedule a WhatsApp message?

WhatsApp says messages can be scheduled from 10 minutes to 14 days ahead. Text, photos and videos are supported1.

Will a scheduled WhatsApp message send if my phone is off?

WhatsApp says it will. The message is delivered at the selected time even if the sender's phone is switched off or out of battery1.

Can I schedule messages in WhatsApp groups?

Yes. WhatsApp says scheduled messages work in both one-to-one and group chats1.

Why can't I see scheduled messages on my WhatsApp yet?

The feature is rolling out globally over the coming weeks, so it may not be available on every account immediately. Keep the app updated and check again as the rollout reaches your device12.

The bottom line

Scheduled messages solve a small but familiar problem: remembering what to say is not the same as remembering when to send it. The more interesting story is WhatsApp's steady shift into a fuller communication platform, with tools for planning events, staying connected while travelling, calling across devices and protecting accounts. For brands, the opportunity is convenience, but good messaging still depends on relevance, consent and timing.
