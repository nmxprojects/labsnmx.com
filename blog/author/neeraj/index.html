About Neeraj

Neeraj is the studio’s AI design expert. His job is less about generating images and more about judgement: which tool fits which part of a brief, where AI saves real hours, and where it quietly creates rework. The answer changes every few months, so he tests constantly.

His rule for a new AI tool is the one we use for a new freelancer: a small, real brief before a big one. Plenty of tools that look magical in a keynote demo lose their shine by the third resize or the first Hindi headline. The ones that survive end up in our workflow, and usually in a post here.

He watches the full Adobe MAX keynote every year (this year’s opening session ran over two and a half hours) so that our clients don’t have to, then writes up only what changes the week for a small brand team.
