About Anisha

Anisha leads market research at NMX Labs. Before a logo sketch or an ad concept, she maps the category: who the competitors are, what they promise, what customers actually complain about, and which claims would need proof.

Her posts are the ones with the fine print. She has written up the declarations Indian packaging must carry under the Legal Metrology rules, how trademark classes and fees work for founders, what Meta’s “AI info” labels mean for ads, and how logo pricing in India really breaks down, built from the quotes clients bring to us.

If a brief has a number, a law or a competitor in it, it has usually been past Anisha first. That habit is why our creative rarely has to be pulled after launch.
