Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information NMX Labs (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit labsnmx.com (the “site”), how we use it, and the choices you have. NMX Labs is based at D3B, Shyam Kunj, Gurugram, Haryana, India.

1. Information you give us

If you use the project enquiry form, we ask for your name, phone number, email, the package you’re interested in and a short brief. The form doesn’t store anything on our servers: it opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled, and they reach us only if you send that message. If you contact us by WhatsApp, email, phone or Instagram, we receive whatever you choose to share. We use this information only to reply to you and to discuss or deliver work you ask about.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and collects information like your device, browser, pages viewed and approximate location. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

3. Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) to measure the results of our ads and to show relevant ads to people who have visited the site. The pixel records actions such as page views, clicks on contact links and enquiry form submissions (not the contents of your enquiry). You can manage how Meta uses this data in your Meta ad preferences.

4. Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

For more on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Embedded videos

Our videos page and some posts embed YouTube videos in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may still set cookies once you play a video. Embedded videos are covered by Google’s privacy policy.

6. Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, the site will still work, but ads may be less relevant and our analytics less complete. Most browsers also let you send a “Do Not Track” signal or browse privately.

7. Sharing

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above, as needed to run the site, or when the law requires it.

8. Retention and security

We keep enquiry messages for as long as we need them to work with you, and delete them on request. Analytics and advertising data are retained according to Google’s and Meta’s own settings and policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or messaging service is completely secure.

9. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent you’ve given, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other laws that apply to you. Email us and we’ll respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children

This site is meant for businesses and adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
