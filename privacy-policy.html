Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what information NMX Labs (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit labsnmx.com (the “site”), how we use it, and the choices you have. NMX Labs is based at D3B, Shyam Kunj, Gurugram, Haryana, India.

1. Information you give us

If you use the project enquiry form, we ask for your name, phone number, email, the package you’re interested in and a short brief. The form doesn’t store anything on our servers: it opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled, and they reach us only if you send that message. If you contact us by WhatsApp, email, phone or Instagram, we receive whatever you choose to share. We use this information only to reply to you and to discuss or deliver work you ask about.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use the site, such as which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and collects information like your device, browser, pages viewed and approximate location. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

3. Meta Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) to measure the results of our ads and to show relevant ads to people who have visited the site. The pixel records actions such as page views, clicks on links and buttons, which blog posts are opened, how far you scroll, how long a page stays open, theme changes and enquiry or newsletter sign-ups (not the contents of your enquiry or your email address). You can manage how Meta uses this data in your Meta ad preferences.

4. Advertising

This site includes Google AdSense code so that Google can review the site and, once approved, show ads on it. When ads are shown, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

For more on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Listening and summaries on blog posts

Blog posts have a Listen button that reads the article aloud using your browser’s built-in speech feature. The text is processed by your device or browser, not sent to us. The “Summarise with Google AI” link opens Google’s AI Mode in a new tab with a request to summarise that article; the article’s address is sent to Google as part of a normal Google search, under Google’s own privacy policy. We don’t send Google anything about you.

Blog newsletter

If you subscribe to the blog, we use your email address only to send you new posts. Depending on how the site is set up at the time, your address, the page you subscribed from and the date are either stored in our private, access-controlled database, or delivered to our inbox (nmxprojects@gmail.com) by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, you can also unsubscribe at labsnmx.com/unsubscribe, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser only.

Comments on blog posts

If you comment on a post, we store your name, email address, comment, the post and the time in our private, access-controlled database. Your name and comment are shown on the post after we review them; your email address is never shown and is used only to reply to you about your comment. Comments are removed on request: email nmxprojects@gmail.com with the post link.

Browser notifications

If you turn on notifications (the Follow button, or the offer shown while you read a post or lesson), your browser gives us a subscription address and two encryption keys created by your browser’s push service (Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft, depending on your browser). We store them in our private, access-controlled database, with the topics you picked (blog posts, YouTube videos), your browser, operating system and device type, an approximate country worked out from your device’s time zone and language, the page you subscribed on, how you first reached the site (the referring website or campaign tags in the link), a random visitor ID, and your email address if you also subscribed to the newsletter in the same browser. We use this only to send the notifications you asked for and to count how many are delivered and tapped. Notifications are sent from our own sending service through your browser’s push service. Right after you subscribe, your device shows one welcome notification so you can check that notifications actually arrive; whether you confirm it, and whether your device blocked it, is recorded with your visitor ID so we can fix delivery problems. Turn notifications off any time from Follow or your browser’s site settings; we then stop sending, and subscriptions that no longer work are marked as expired. Ask us to delete your record by email.

Visitor statistics

Besides Google Analytics, the site sends us basic statistics about each page view: the page, the referring website, campaign tags in the link, device type, browser, operating system, language, an approximate country from your time zone, and a random visitor ID kept in your browser’s local storage (not a cookie, and not linked to your name). We also record actions such as opening the assistant, saving a post or installing the app. These rows are kept for 120 days in our private, access-controlled database. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, page views aren’t recorded.

Ask NMX Labs assistant

The chat assistant answers common questions from information on this site. Other questions, and the last few messages of your conversation, are sent to Google’s Gemini API through our own server to write an answer; Google processes them under its Gemini API terms, which for free use allow Google to use the content to improve its products. Don’t share sensitive personal information in the chat. We keep the questions and answers in our private database to improve the site. If you use “Talk to a person”, we store your name, contact details and message, email them to ourselves, and use them only to reply to you.

Saved posts, offline reading and the app

Posts you save, and pages you’ve opened recently, are stored only in your browser so they open without a connection. They are never sent to us. You can install the site as an app; it’s the same website and collects nothing extra. Clearing your browser’s site data removes all of this.

Creator school lessons, quizzes and games

Your lesson progress, quiz answers, best scores and Daily five streak are stored only in your browser’s local storage and are never sent to us with your answers. We count, with the same random visitor ID as our visitor statistics, when someone finishes a lesson, a quiz (with the score) or a game, so we know which lessons and games are useful. Clearing your browser’s site data resets your progress.

News feed

The News page loads headlines from Google News and publisher RSS feeds, either through our own server or through rss2json.com. Your browser contacts that service to load headlines; we don’t send it anything about you.

6. AMP versions of blog posts

Our blog posts also have AMP (Accelerated Mobile Pages) versions that may be served from Google’s AMP cache when you open them from Google Search. These pages use the same Google Analytics and Google AdSense services described above.

7. Embedded videos

Our videos page and some posts embed YouTube videos in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube may still set cookies once you play a video. Embedded videos are covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Social and news feeds

Our news page and blog show our latest Instagram and X posts and a list of headlines from other publishers. Instagram posts and headlines are fetched by our server and shown as links; clicking one takes you to Instagram or the publisher’s site, which have their own privacy policies. If we show X’s embedded timeline, it is loaded from X (twitter.com) and X may set cookies or collect information about your visit under its own privacy policy. We don’t share any of your information with these services.

8. Cookies and your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, the site will still work, but ads may be less relevant and our analytics less complete. Most browsers also let you send a “Do Not Track” signal or browse privately.

9. Sharing

Personalisation and your choices

Where you are. To show posts, news and reading picked for your part of the world, the site works out your likely country from your device’s time zone setting (for example “Asia/Kolkata” means India). We never use your IP address for this, and you can change the region yourself under “Personalise what you see” at the bottom of any page.

What you read. Your browser keeps a short note of which topics you open, how many posts you read and finish, and the hours you usually visit, so the site can pick posts for you. It stays on your device. Once a day it sends us a summary with your random visitor ID: likely country and time zone, languages, device type, operating system, browser, screen size, light or dark mode, data-saver and connection type, whether you installed the app or turned on notifications, the number of visits and posts read, your top topics, your usual hours and how you first and last arrived (for example “Instagram · Meta ads”). It contains no IP address, no name and no contact details. Nothing is sent when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

What you tell us. The “Personalise” sheet asks for topics, what you do, the region you want, how often and how you want to hear from us, and optionally your first name, email address, brand or company and WhatsApp number. These are saved on your device; they are sent to us only if you tick the consent box. We use them to personalise the site and to send you only what you asked for, on the channels you picked. If you choose email, we add you to the email list and send a welcome email. We contact you on WhatsApp only if you tick WhatsApp and give your number. Choose “Clear my choices” in the same sheet at any time and we delete them; or email us and we will delete everything we hold about you.

Prices in your currency. On the services page, readers outside India see an approximate price in their currency. Your browser loads today’s exchange rates from open.er-api.com to do this; it sends nothing about you.

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers named above (Google, which stores our data and runs the Gemini API; our website host; Meta; FormSubmit; open.er-api.com for currency rates, which receives no information about you; and your browser’s push service), as needed to run the site, or when the law requires it.

10. Retention and security

We keep enquiry messages for as long as we need them to work with you, and delete them on request. Analytics and advertising data are retained according to Google’s and Meta’s own settings and policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website or messaging service is completely secure.

11. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw consent you’ve given, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other laws that apply to you. Email us and we’ll respond within a reasonable time.

12. Children

This site is meant for businesses and adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
