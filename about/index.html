About NMX Labs

NMX Labs is a creative and performance marketing studio based in Gurugram, India. We design brand identities, social and campaign creative, and run Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads, either as standalone projects or as one integrated creative and media system.

Who we work with

D2C brands, EV and energy companies, SaaS, hospitality and creator-led businesses, from pre-launch to post-Series A, in India and abroad. Brands we’ve worked with include iNvent (an Apple Premium Partner), Miah Shades, Luna Organic Sanitary Napkins and Dioré. See more on our clients page.

How we work

We’re a small, senior team. There’s no account-management layer and no juniors doing the real work behind a senior pitch. Pricing is fixed upfront, so scope is a conversation rather than a surprise, and we reply on WhatsApp and Slack quickly.

We use AI tools where they make the work better and faster, and keep them out of the places where accuracy and ownership matter, like logos, product labels and claims. We’ve written up exactly how that works.

Why we write a blog

Because we run campaigns every week, we see what works on real budgets. The blog is where we share it: tool news explained for brand teams, our processes, and practical guides on branding and Meta ads.

Where we are

NMX Labs, D3B, Shyam Kunj, Gurugram, Haryana, India. You can reach us on WhatsApp, email or through the contact page.

Services and pricing

Branding and identity from ₹25,000. Social media creative from ₹8,000 a month. Campaign creative from ₹10,000. Meta Ads management at ₹8,000 a month plus ad spend, or ₹15,000 a month bundled with creative. Brand and marketing design from ₹7,500. See all services.
